When a sentence cites results from a table or figure, use the passive construction,
"As reported in Table~\ref{tab:X}, ..." / "as shown in Figure~\ref{fig:Y}", NOT the
active "As Table~\ref{tab:X} reports, ..." (user, 2026-07-31, Water and Crop).

**Why:** the economics literature convention is passive voice for exhibit citations;
the active form with the exhibit as subject reads off-register.

**How to apply:** all projects, all economics manuscripts. This refines the
`~/.claude/rules/cross-references.md` suggestion of "as Figure X demonstrates/shows"
— prefer the passive variant ("as demonstrated/shown in Figure X"). Natural-sentence
refs where the exhibit is genuinely the subject of a non-results statement
("Table 3 lists the districts") remain fine. Related: [[feedback-no-section-references-in-prose]].
