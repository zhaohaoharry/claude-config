When the Carbon Pricing Policy paper was retargeted to JDE (2026-11-18), the user objected to "developing" placed
again and again (abstract, intro, keywords). The paper studies carbon pricing across the world; the JDE angle is the
consequence for the unregulated part of the world, which is mostly developing. Say "countries with laxer climate
policies" / "unregulated countries" / "non-pricing countries" in framing, and "developing" only where it is the
income-group result (the high-income vs developing split) or stated once as a fact. Not a keyword. Also no "rich":
the adopters are not all high-income (22 of 38 under the FY1998 World Bank groups).

**Why:** "we don't want to intentionally put the word; we want to fill in the idea." Repeating the label reads as
forced journal fitting.

**How to apply:** for any journal retargeting, convey the angle through substance and the choice of contrast, not by
inserting the journal's buzzword. Related: [[feedback_journal_angle_not_new_claim]].
