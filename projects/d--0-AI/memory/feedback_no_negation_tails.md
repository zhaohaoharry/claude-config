In manuscript prose, state what is done and stop. Do not append a clause that says what it is not, such as
"the potential of an active link is the one observed from its share, **and it is not predicted**". The user
deleted that clause on 2026-11-13 (Carbon Pricing Policy, Section 5.3) with "this is not needed. please avoid
writing like this in the future".

**Why:** the positive statement already carries the fact; the negation only answers a contrast the reader did not
raise. It is the sentence-level form of defensive writing, see [[feedback_no_loose_sentences]] and
[[feedback_no_footprints_of_abandoned_designs]].

**How to apply:** before saving a sentence, delete any tail of the form "and it is not X", "rather than X",
"not Y" when X or Y was not already in the text. Applies to all manuscripts. In chat explanations a contrast is
fine when the user asked about the distinction. (The scope beyond manuscripts is my interpretation.)
