When revising the author's manuscript prose, keep his original sentences and change only what has a stated reason (a wrong term, a redundant sentence, a missing fact). Do not replace a paragraph with a fresh rewrite because it could be phrased better. Stated 2026-09-04 (Governor and Border Emissions) after an earlier pass had rewritten the model's attribution paragraph wholesale: "I want to avoid rewrite if unnecessary and only change the things need change." Earlier the same day, for the background restructure: "try not change my previous writing too much, but make sure to remove redundant and unnecessary discussions."

**Why:** the author's own wording is his voice and his checked content. A wholesale rewrite forces him to re-verify every claim, hides which change was the necessary one, and produces long del/add pairs that defeat the redline.

**How to apply:** for each proposed change, be able to name the defect it fixes (term inconsistency, redundancy, inaccuracy, missing definition, broken flow). If none, leave the sentence. Express fixes as minimal-span marks inside the original sentence. Reserve full-sentence replacement for sentences whose claim is wrong.
