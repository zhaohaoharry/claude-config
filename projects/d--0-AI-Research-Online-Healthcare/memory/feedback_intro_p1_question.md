The user's general preference for introductions: paragraph 1 opens with the economic debate the paper speaks to and states the research question in that same paragraph. Every later paragraph then serves that question. One message per paragraph, and the question is asked once.

**Why:** on 2026-10-26 the user rejected an introduction that reached "This paper asks..." in paragraph 5, after four motivation paragraphs. They called it "way too late".

**How to apply:** for every paper:
- Give the user an outline of the intended message of each paragraph before drafting any prose.
- In P1, move from the debate to the question.
- Then motivation and setting, design, results (one paragraph each), contribution, closest paper, roadmap.
