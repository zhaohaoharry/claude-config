On 2026-11-23 the user decided that the manuscript and its generated tables and figures must not name the consultation platform. It is introduced once as "a large online consultation platform in China" and is "the platform" after that. Superlatives such as "largest" and identifying details such as the founding year are left out. The appendix figure of archived haodf.com screenshots (fig:haodf, files fig_haodf_*.png) was removed. Chen et al. (2022) are described as studying "the same platform".

**Why:** the data were shared by the platform long ago, and the user does not want the platform to claim credit for the research or to raise objections. The source will still be disclosed to the journal in the data statement.

**How to apply:** never write "Haodf" in manuscript text, table notes, figure labels or captions. Internal code comments and file names may keep it. Grep the compiled PDF for the name before sharing.
