For the Online Healthcare manuscript, the user judged prose built from 40-word cumulative sentences (chains of since/while/which clauses, every sentence the same length) as "not like human writing". The reference voice is the authors' own draft `latex/HospitalChoice.tex`. It uses plain declaratives of about 15 to 25 words, one idea per sentence, an explicit question ("How much of the observed home bias ... is driven by preference ...?"), "First/Second/Third" signposting, and "we" as the subject. After each number it says what the number means.

**Why:** the user's reaction on 2026-10-25, after reading a draft written to the global guide's 40-word target. It overrides that target for this project.

**How to apply:**
- Vary sentence length and keep one idea per sentence.
- Use short sentences for questions and turns ("Why do buyers favor home?", "Online consultations do.").
- Avoid HospitalChoice's own hype words: novel, striking, massive, natural laboratory, deep parameters.
- Keep the surface rules: no colons, semicolons or em-dashes.
